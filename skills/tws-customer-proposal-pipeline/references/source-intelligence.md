# Source Intelligence

Use this when building the customer background and latest-signal section.

## Browse Rules

- Always verify current information for modern companies, executives, locations, products, news, ERP/MES, ESG, and expansion plans.
- Open the user-provided URL first.
- Search official company pages next: company profile, news, sustainability/ESG, investor relations, factory/location pages, product pages.
- Use credible news or industry sources for context, but separate facts from inference.
- Use exact dates in notes and slide footnotes, especially for article dates, announced factory schedules, or project milestones.

## Extract These Signals

Create a compact source table while researching:

- `source`: URL or local file path.
- `date`: article/publication/update date.
- `fact`: concrete statement.
- `proposal signal`: why it matters to TWS.
- `confidence`: high / medium / low.

Focus on:

- Industry and product family.
- Factory sites, new plant plans, warehouse moves, cross-site operations.
- ERP/MES/WMS/Dashboard/BI/AI/digital transformation.
- ESG, SBTi, ISO 14067, ISO 50001, green factory, carbon footprint, energy management.
- Labor, accuracy, traceability, EDI/customer requirements, export packing, shipping marks.
- SKU complexity, small-parts handling, WIP, line-side supply, dock/staging, pallet flow.

## Source Handling

- Do not quote long passages. Paraphrase.
- Do not invent metrics or exact constraints.
- If a data point is uncertain, phrase it as a meeting confirmation item.
- Add a quiet footnote on relevant slides such as:
  `註：依 <source> <YYYY-MM-DD> 公開資訊整理；實際方案需以現場盤點為準。`

## Customer Intelligence Output

Before making slides, summarize:

- Customer trigger: what changed recently.
- Likely operating issue: why material flow matters.
- TWS entry point: first services to discuss.
- Suggested first meeting: what to confirm.
