# TWS Business Development Deck Rules

Use this when generating a TWS/TWSC/奔騰物流 customer-facing business
development deck (new factory, building-permit lead, cold outreach, warehouse
automation, logistics improvement, integrated proposal).

## Contents

- Master direction and engagement modes
- Existing-customer and new-factory structures
- Module selection heuristics
- Customer-facing language
- Visual pattern
- Validation

Asset paths referenced below (v0.7 master direction, service-scope overlay set,
logos) are all defined in `assets-manifest.md`. Governance and copy/numeric
discipline are in `shared-governance.md` + `../data/banned_terms.json`.

## Current Master Direction (v0.7)

A low-pressure business conversation deck with enough TWS company background
plus the complete service-scope asset set (clean no-text scene + editable PPTX
label overlays). The deck must feel like a customer-specific invitation:

- 8–10 slides for first-touch development (10 when the customer needs
  company/equipment context).
- Editable PPTX first; PDF only on explicit request.
- Customer-facing language from slide 1; no internal workflow or review-status
  text; no product-catalog dumping.
- Human title style: short, direct, and customer-spoken. Avoid abstract
  proposal labels such as `改善主軸`, `整體方案`, `導入路徑`, `方案架構`, or
  `解決方案藍圖`.
- Each slide carries one short title and one visual or flow. Attach two to four
  labels to the visual; keep the explanation for the speaker. Concentrate long
  confirmation lists into one meeting page instead of scattering checklist
  fragments across the deck.
- Goal: a 30–60 minute conversation, not acceptance of a technical plan.

## Engagement Mode Decision

Do not force every deck into the new-factory cold-development structure:

- `陌生開發`: customer background, public trigger, TWS role, service scope,
  soft meeting invitation.
- `既有客戶改善提案`: minimal background; start from known pain points, site
  scope, improvement targets, recommended equipment, confirmation items,
  staged next actions.
- `客戶決策版`: for managers/engineering/procurement — problem-to-solution
  logic, tracking items, condition boundaries, clear next-step table. Must be
  globally consistent: no unmarked alternative equipment paths, scope
  definitions, WMS/WCS boundaries, or aisle/height/load/throughput
  assumptions; label alternatives and state what decides between them.
- `設備選型版`: why the equipment fits the scenario, what must be confirmed,
  what is out of scope. Official/user images beat generated visuals.
- `內部規劃版`: only on explicit request.

For existing-customer or decision decks, cut public customer-news pages: the
customer needs the proposal mapped to their site, pain, constraints, and next
decision — not to be told who they are.

## Existing-Customer Improvement Deck Structure (~12 slides)

1. Cover — customer, site/scope, direct improvement theme; no news framing.
2. Proposal focus — one table/diagram: site / pain / direction / tracking item.
3. Current pain and improvement goals (operational language).
4. Overall improvement flow — storage, equipment, movement, WMS/status return.
5–8. Site or equipment modules — one per site/process/equipment family, with
   authority product images, fit reason, confirmation conditions.
9. System / WMS boundary — what is included now vs future extension; do not
   imply replacing existing systems unless requested.
10. Deployment order — `第一步 / 第二步 / 第三步`, each tied to a meeting
   output or verification condition.
11. Confirmation items — customer confirms dimensions/flow/frequency/site/
   interfaces; TWS provides configuration direction, layout, movement review,
   budget estimate.
12. Next step — concrete post-meeting deliverables.

## Default New-Factory Deck Structure (10 slides)

1. Cover — customer name, soft theme (`新廠物流效率交流提案`), TWS identity,
   3–4 public clue metrics.
2. Why talk now — trigger as business reason; permits are context only;
   building completion ≠ logistics readiness, early alignment reduces rework.
3. Initial operating context — known facts; mark inferences `初步觀察`; no raw
   source rows or internal gaps; no homework requests yet.
4. Common new-factory logistics bottlenecks — `空間 / 流程 / 人力 / 系統`,
   phrased as `常見` / `容易`, never `貴司一定有`.
5. TWS company role — integrated partner since 1980; concise, not a history
   deck.
6. TWS service and equipment modules — reuse the v0.7 overlay master (slide 1
   for most proposals, slide 2 for full range, slide 3 as no-text base; paths
   in `assets-manifest.md`). Keep labels/leader lines/brands editable. Six
   module families in business language: `運搬與車隊`, `垂直倉儲`,
   `月台與裝卸`, `料架與儲位`, `自動化與密集庫`, `清潔、系統與維運`. Lead with
   the corrected product lines (Toyota MH group, Modula, MBB, Galaxis, AiTEN,
   Geek+, racks, FastLINK, LionsBot); explain the operating issue each module
   helps with, no catalog dumping or hard performance claims.
7. Integrated TWS logistics map — five layers `進出貨 / 儲存 / 搬運 / 系統 /
   維運`; caption `先看整張地圖，再決定要先動哪一塊。`
8. Low-pressure conversation path — `第一次交流` → `第二步盤點` → `後續方案`;
   reassurance `現在先聊，不代表立刻採購。`
9. TWS capability proof — concise: since 1980, thousands of customers,
   planning + equipment + automation + software + installation + training +
   maintenance.
10. Next step — 30–60 minute discussion; headline
   `先不用決定設備，只需要一起確認新廠物流現場的基本輪廓。`; technical details
   as `會議可一起確認`.

## Module Selection Heuristics

- Large floor area / warehouse use → layout, rack density, dock flow, forklift
  fleet, WMS readiness.
- High clear height / VNA likely → VNA, floor flatness/load, rack design,
  sprinkler/beam checks.
- Cold chain / food / pharma / chemical → dock sealing, cold/rapid doors,
  hygiene, traceability, WMS, safety.
- Electronics / precision / line-side production → line-side storage, VLM,
  AGV/AMR, WMS/MES connection, small-parts picking, safety.
- Logistics / 3PL → throughput, storage density, staging, WMS/WCS,
  shuttle/ASRS, dock interfaces.
- Sparse data → conservative recommendations; next step is a diagnostic
  meeting.

## Customer-Facing Language

Preferred phrasings:

- `依目前公開資料與建廠線索，TWS 建議先從以下方向確認。`
- `初步觀察：本案的價值不在單一設備，而在新廠投產前把儲位、動線、設備與系統一次對齊。`
- `會議確認項目：棧板尺寸、日出入庫量、SKU 數、梁下淨高、地坪荷重、投產時程。`
- `建議下一步：以 30-60 分鐘會議完成作業輪廓盤點，再判斷是否需要圖面配置或現場勘查。`
- `我們先從交流開始，不急著談設備清單。` / `先聊，不代表立刻採購。`
- `新廠投產前，倉儲物流規劃越早對齊，後續返工機率越低。`
- `本階段先做狀態回報，不更換既有系統。`
- `完成現場條件確認後，TWS 可提供配置圖、動線初評與預算估算。`

Banned/discouraged terms are machine-readable in `../data/banned_terms.json`
(scanned by `qa_check.py`). Naming: use `改善建議書` / `改善提案` /
`設備選型與導入建議` / `客戶決策版`; reserve `白皮書` for research-style
internal documents.

## Visual Pattern

- Strong cover with customer name and direct offer; cover image and Image 2.0
  style rules in `shared-governance.md`. Default generated visuals should use
  the low-realism flat illustration style so they sit naturally beside editable
  system maps and process diagrams.
- One short title per slide; prefer system maps, timelines, flows, before/after
  views, and equipment visuals. Keep labels editable and short.
- Prefer fewer, clearer modules over complete coverage. If a slide starts to
  read like an equipment inventory or a written report, replace the text with
  a diagram or move the detail to speaker notes/appendix.
- Brighter business style for cold outreach: warm white background, deep green
  titles, white cards, warm gold metrics, very limited red (tokens in
  `scripts/tws_pptx.py`).
- TWS logos and relevant local product/project images (paths in
  `assets-manifest.md`).
- Polished but operational: fewer decorative blocks, more readable diagrams
  and practical checks.

## Validation

Run `qa_check.py` plus the QA level from `shared-governance.md`. Additional
business checks for this deck type:

- 8–10 slides for first-touch development (per the v0.7 master direction).
- No hard claims invented from weak data.
- The next step is concrete enough for a customer to accept a meeting.
- Slides read as a finished customer proposal, not a report about the
  proposal.
