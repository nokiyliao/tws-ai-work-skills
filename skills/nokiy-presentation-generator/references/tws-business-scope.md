## Core Positioning

TWS / Taiwan Warehouse Solution Corp. / 奔騰物流系統 should be positioned as:

- A warehouse and logistics integrated solution planner and execution partner.
- A system integrator that connects equipment, space, process, software, safety,
  after-sales service, and financial deployment options.
- A practical implementation partner with roots from 1980, material-handling
  equipment experience, racking and installation capability, automation
  integration, WMS experience, and after-sales service.

Preferred positioning sentence:

`TWS helps customers turn warehouse space, material flow, equipment, software, and service into one maintainable operating system.`

## Integrated Capability Map

Think in layers rather than product categories.

### 1. Site, Flow, And Dock Interface

Scope:

- Loading dock planning.
- FastLINK dock/loading interface equipment.
- MBB vehicle/dock loading interface and tail-lift/loading support where
  relevant.
- Dock levelers, dock shelters/seals, industrial doors, rapid doors.
- Inbound/outbound staging, truck interface, cold-room doors, safety at doors.
- Conveyors, extendable conveyors, cross-floor or cross-zone transfer.

Customer value:

- Faster loading/unloading.
- Less waiting at dock.
- Cleaner cold-chain or temperature-control boundary.
- Safer vehicle/person/equipment interface.

### 2. Storage Structure And Floor

Scope:

- Heavy-duty pallet racks, selective racks, drive-in racks, push-back racks.
- Narrow aisle and VNA rack planning.
- Radio shuttle, two-way shuttle, four-way shuttle, dense storage rack systems.
- Floor load, flatness, abrasion, VNA DIN requirements, slab renovation.
- CAD layout, pallet-position planning, aisle width and clear-height checks.

Customer value:

- Higher storage density.
- Safer high-lift or narrow-aisle operation.
- Earlier detection of floor/load/height constraints.
- More reliable installation and acceptance conditions.

### 3. Material Handling And Fleet

Scope:

- Toyota Material Handling group brands including Toyota and BT forklifts,
  reach trucks, pallet trucks, and VNA trucks.
- Manual, semi-automatic, and automated material handling.
- Equipment rental, replacement, maintenance, and fleet management.
- TOYOTA I_Site style vehicle/personnel/impact/utilization monitoring.
- PPS/personnel protection and vehicle safety systems.

Customer value:

- Stable daily handling capacity.
- Reduced labor fatigue and waiting.
- Better vehicle utilization and accident visibility.
- Safer people-equipment separation.

### 4. Automation And High-Density Systems

Scope:

- Modula VLM / vertical lift modules.
- AiTEN and Geek+ style AGV/AMR/Keycart/LGV material transport.
- Goods-to-person, sortation, electronic picking labels, robotic support.
- Pallet AS/RS, mini-load AS/RS, two-way shuttle, four-way shuttle.
- Galaxis and Geek+ style dense-storage platforms with WMS/WCS integration.

Customer value:

- Reduced low-value repetitive movement.
- Higher space utilization.
- Higher picking accuracy and throughput.
- More scalable phased investment than all-or-nothing automation.

### 5. Warehouse Software And Data

Scope:

- WMS, WCS, barcode/RF/PDA workflows.
- Inventory, inbound, outbound, transfer, picking, packing, cycle count.
- Integration with ERP/MES, automation equipment, conveyors, shuttles, VLM.
- TMS/TOS routing or dispatch optimization where relevant.
- Data collection for operation visibility and continuous improvement.

Customer value:

- Fewer manual decisions.
- More transparent inventory and task progress.
- Better equipment/system orchestration.
- Stronger auditability and management control.

### 6. Safety, Cleaning, Access, And Site Support

Scope:

- LionsBot autonomous floor scrubbers and industrial cleaning automation.
- Aerial work platforms, wrapping machines, guards, access support.
- Safety monitoring, warning lines, barriers, traffic separation.
- Site service, maintenance, training, parts, phased implementation support.

Customer value:

- Safer and cleaner operating environment.
- Lower routine labor pressure.
- Easier facility maintenance and inspection readiness.

## Common Customer Issues And TWS Translation

- `倉庫不夠用` -> storage density, rack layout, VNA, shuttle, VLM, AS/RS.
- `找貨慢 / 揀貨錯` -> WMS, barcode/PDA, electronic labels, VLM, goods-to-person.
- `搬運人力不足` -> forklifts, AGV/AMR, conveyors, route optimization.
- `出入庫塞車` -> dock planning, staging zones, WMS dispatch, conveyors,
  flow redesign.
- `高架或窄巷安全疑慮` -> floor flatness/load, VNA requirements, rack design,
  traffic separation, PPS.
- `新廠快投產` -> early technical checks: layout, clear height, fire/mechanical,
  floor, dock, power, system integration, installation schedule.
- `冷鏈/食品/醫藥` -> dock sealing, rapid/cold doors, hygiene, traceability,
  WMS, controlled staging.
- `產線補料或製造物流` -> line-side storage, AGV/AMR, VLM, WMS/MES interface.

## Customer-Facing Claim Discipline

In customer-facing decks:

- Tie every recommended module to a customer operating issue.
- Use `初步觀察` or `建議確認條件` for assumptions.
- Do not invent hard ROI, storage counts, delivery dates, equipment quantities,
  prices, or compliance claims.
- If a source deck has old figures, use non-numeric proof unless the user gives
  the current verified number.
- Present TWS as the party that can diagnose and integrate; do not pretend the
  first cold outreach has enough data for final design.
